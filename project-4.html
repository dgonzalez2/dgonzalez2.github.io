Pacific Control: El Ultimo Mandato

Tense, chaotic city management Rogue-like game made for the LAGS Game Jam 2026!

Project Overview

Pacific Control is a 1 week game jam project I worked on with a group of talented friends for the Latin American Games Showcase Game Jam 2026. It was second time joining a group of developers to work on a common goal and brainstorm a game that we can make within a reasonable time frame. It was a lot of fun to talk and worked in to the very late nights on this project. This game was also popular enough that it reached the top 10 in originality and top 20 in the overall rankings from a total of 109 submissions!
Pacific Control: El Ultimo Mandato is a 3D city management game with Rogue-like elements where you work as a dictator trying to keep control over the country you took over. But to do this you must managed finite resources over a large area and keep track of the health of each of the zones because if too many cities rebel against you or are destroyed in the process you lose and due to the chaos of the game, the player must work blindly to guess how to take care of the city with nothing but a few newspaper reals and a very chatty reporter.
This game has been developed with me and 4 others. I was in charge of the resource management mechanic in the game. This part included make the events and systems that would lead to each city zone detecting resource tokens, calculate the values of each of the zones stats and making sure that at the end of each turn instantiating more tokens as needed based on how far along the player was in the game. It also included what to do when a zones stats were low enough to invock a losing flag.

Features

At it's core Pacific Control: El Ultimo Mandato features:
-A top-down 3D perspective
-A newspaper system to see each zone's stats
-Dynamic News Caster to tell players what is going on
-Mutiple values to keep track
-Different zones to unlock
-Zones to be dynamically destoyed based on the lowest values
-Roguelight elements that intesify each round
