Baby Clinic

A nursing tycoon simulator on Roblox

Project Overview

Baby Clinic is the big project I became in charge during my time at FiveBits Studios and a true test to see how well I could hold in a profesional envinroment. This project is a culmination of my time and effort of my almost year long career with them.
Baby Clinic is a fun simulator where players take the role of 3 different doctors all of which are charge of taking care of babies are they being born while being careful to not let the line into the Clinic not get too long.
This project was developed by me and a team of 3 other people where I worked as the both the director and lead programmer while the others served as the artists and programmers in the project. This project was certainly challenging as using the roblox platform for development was a challenge in of itself. Unlike Unity, Godot, Unreal or other engines the develeopment platform for Roblox can seem very bare and while some practices can work on some engines using this platform required a different mindset. This project took a few months to release in which despite being the lead and director on this project, my team included someone who was very experienced in roblox who helped greatly in helping with the more intricate parts of the project. Despite this being my final project in the internship I was very greatful for the opportunity.

Features

At it's core Baby Clinic features:
-3D environment
-3 different roles -Money based progression system
-Can be with friends up to 8 players
-A colorful artstyle and custom made outfits -A lot of fun with physics based items
