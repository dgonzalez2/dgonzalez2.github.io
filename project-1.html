Haley Is Not Alone

A simple survival horror experience built using Godot and Aseprite

Project Overview

Haley Is Not Alone has been my attempt to being able to make something bigger and a name for mysekf with the skills I have learned in univeristy. This game came about during the Unity run-time fee controversy where I believed it became time for me to learn how to use other other engines besides Unity.
Haley Is Not Alone can be best described a small survival horror experience where you explore different locations, solve different puzzles and fight to survive a world gone mad over night. This game has a demo that will soon be releaased on Steam and Itch.io.
This game has been developed entirely on my own as the sole developer on this project. First having to learn the ins and outs of a new progam that used ECS instead of OOP like Unity. Working on this title became much easier than I realized thanks to my previous work at FiveBits Studios because Roblox also uses similar principles in its design philosophy. This game also taught me alot in time management and planning as I initially believed such a small and compact game would take a lot less time than I believed. But thankfully I am now fully on track being able to make this game into the big step foward of my career.

Features

At it's core Haley Is Not Alone features:
-A top-down 2D perspective
-8-bit graphics
-3 Open world envinronments
-3 distinct weapons with different specialities -A distinct black and white art style -A story that is slowly unfolds over time as the player progresses
